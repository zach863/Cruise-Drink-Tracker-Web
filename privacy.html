Summary

  • Local app information: Information you enter in Cruise Drink Tracker is stored locally on your device.
  • No tracking: Cruise Drink Tracker does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • Website: cruisedrinktracker.com does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers, or contact forms.

Owner and Operator

Cruise Drink Tracker is owned and operated by Diamond JZ Ventures LLC. Questions about this policy or the product may be sent to info@cruisedrinktracker.com.

Information Stored Locally in the App

Cruise Drink Tracker stores information you enter locally on your device, including drink logs, trip or cruise settings, traveler information, Favorites, Try List items, notes, ratings, venues, saved cruise history or snapshots, and app settings. This local app information is not the same as information collected by Diamond JZ Ventures LLC. Based on the current app's behavior, Diamond JZ Ventures LLC does not receive this information.

Photos

You may choose a photo from your library or take a photo with your camera for Drinks, Favorites, or Try List items. The app stores its own local copies of those images on your device. You may also explicitly save a Cruise or Day summary image to Photos. The app does not automatically upload these photos to Diamond JZ Ventures LLC.

Share to Try List

If you explicitly share text, URLs, or images to Cruise Drink Tracker using Share to Try List, the shared content is kept locally so the app can import it. Diamond JZ Ventures LLC does not receive the shared content.

User-Directed External Requests

If you explicitly share a web image or direct image URL, Cruise Drink Tracker may request that image from the third-party website hosting it. The retrieved image is then processed and stored locally by the app. This request goes to that third-party host; the app does not forward the image to Diamond JZ Ventures LLC.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Cruise Drink Tracker does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs; does not use a developer account system or cloud sync; and does not use tracking identifiers. We do not use information from the app to track you across apps or websites or share it for advertising or measurement purposes.

Apple App Analytics

Apple may provide developers with aggregate App Store or App Analytics information according to Apple's services and settings. This is separate from Cruise Drink Tracker collecting user data itself.

Website Privacy

The Cruise Drink Tracker website at cruisedrinktracker.com is hosted using GitHub Pages. The website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, newsletter integrations, third-party fonts, or third-party JavaScript or content-delivery networks. The website does not use first-party cookies for tracking.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect personal information from any users, we have no such data to use or share.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and update the “Effective date” above.
